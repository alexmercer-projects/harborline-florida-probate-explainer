## Session law

- Chapter 2026-57, Laws of Florida (CS/HB 1337), sections 7 to 10 and 14: raised the summary administration limit from $75,000 to $150,000 and changed the 735.302, 735.303 and 735.304 amounts, effective July 1, 2026. The act does not say whether the new limit depends on the date of death. https://laws.flrules.org/files/Ch_2026-057.pdf

## Constitution and rules

- Florida Constitution, Art. X, s. 4 (homestead exemption): https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Constitution
- Florida Probate Rule 5.030(a): a personal representative must be represented by a Florida attorney unless the personal representative is the sole interested person. Copy published by the Collier County Clerk: https://www.collierclerk.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-01-01-Florida-Probate-Rules-5.030.pdf
